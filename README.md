# cancer-theory

**An open research hypothesis — not medical advice, not a proven cure.**
See [`DISCLAIMER.md`](DISCLAIMER.md).

This repository is a public, in-the-open working space for a scientific hypothesis
about cancer: that a tumour behaves as a **recursive positive-feedback loop** that
feeds on fuel normal cells largely avoid (glutamine, lactate, ketones), strips the
body's dampening and immune forces, and past a threshold **captures host and cell
metabolism** to fuel itself and further suppress immunity.

The goal is explicit: **state the idea clearly, attach real citations, and track
honestly what is supported, unsupported, or contradicted** by published literature —
so it can inform future, properly-designed trials. Nothing here is a treatment
recommendation.

## ⚠️ Important

- This is a **hypothesis under development**, not a validated result.
- **Nothing here is medical advice.** Do not act on it for diagnosis or treatment.
- No claim is settled until its status file says `supported` — and even then that
  means "consistent with published evidence," not "proven in humans."

## Layout

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| [`/theory`](theory) | The hypothesis itself: the loop, the mechanisms, the mathematical model. |
| [`/evidence`](evidence) | Citations and summaries of the published literature each claim rests on. |
| [`/claims`](claims) | One file per claim, each with a **status** and **evidence tier**; see [`claims/INDEX.md`](claims/INDEX.md). |

## Claim vocabulary

- **Status:** `supported` · `partially-supported` · `contradicted` · `unverified` · `hypothesis`
- **Evidence tier:** `human-rct` · `human-phase1-2` · `animal` · `in-vitro` · `theoretical`

Claim status is maintained through independent literature review — see
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md). A claim marked `supported` or
`partially-supported` means it is consistent with the published record, nothing more.
