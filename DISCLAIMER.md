# Disclaimer

This repository documents an **open research hypothesis** about cancer biology.

- It is **not medical advice**, **not a diagnosis or treatment**, and **not a proven cure**.
- No statement here should be acted on for any medical decision.
- Claims are tracked with an explicit **status** and **evidence tier**. A claim is
  only consistent with published evidence once its claim file is marked `supported`
  (or `partially-supported`) by literature review — and even then that means
  "consistent with the published record," never "proven in humans."
- Any entry flagged `medical_claim_flag: true` touches a clinical/therapeutic
  assertion and should be read with particular caution.

The purpose is to state the idea clearly, attach real citations, and track honestly
what holds up — so it can inform future, properly-designed research.
